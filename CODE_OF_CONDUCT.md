# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other place where the project is represented.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticize the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not publish someone's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **kbs4520@daum.net**. Reports are read by the
maintainer, handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept
confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban a contributor
temporarily or permanently for behaviour judged inappropriate, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
